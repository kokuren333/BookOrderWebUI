# Cadence editing

After developmental decisions, inspect sentence and paragraph openings, sentence lengths, transitions, contrast constructions, paragraph endings and explicit logical connectors across adjacent pages. Edit only repetition that makes the argument mechanical or obscures emphasis.

Do not randomize lengths, replace words with synonyms for variety, make formal writing informal, or weaken technical precision. Preserve citations, source fidelity and the established StyleBible voice. Record targeted edits in `plan/prose-editing.yaml`.
