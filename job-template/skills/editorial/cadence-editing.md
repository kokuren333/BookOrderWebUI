# Cadence editing

After developmental decisions, inspect sentence and paragraph openings, sentence lengths, transitions, contrast constructions, paragraph endings and explicit logical connectors across adjacent pages. Edit only repetition that makes the argument mechanical or obscures emphasis.

Do not randomize lengths, replace words with synonyms for variety, change the register, or weaken technical precision. Preserve citations, source fidelity and the voice the user asked for (then the Book Bible voice). If the user wanted rough, first-person, uneven or polemical prose, that is the target: remove only unintended mechanical repetition; never smooth it into balanced, evenly paced explanatory prose. Record targeted edits in `plan/prose-editing.yaml`.
