# Whole-book review

Read every finished chapter in order before deciding. Compare the opening and ending of every chapter and section, repeated transitions, recaps, qualifications, summary structures and arguments. Look for the same rhetorical job performed in the same form across chapters, even when the words differ.

Classify rhetorical roles from context and compare chapter-opening recap and preview rates, summary endings, qualification density and role sequences. Keep those comparisons separate from lexical n-grams, prefixes and repeated phrases. Mark exact locations and explain the reader cost of each repetition. A repeated form may be requested by the user, deliberate pedagogy or a genre convention; keep it when the user
asked for it or when it advances comprehension. Feed candidates to `plan/prose-audit.yaml`. This skill diagnoses the complete book and does not edit chapters.
