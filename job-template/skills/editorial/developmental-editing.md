# Developmental editing

Work from `plan/prose-audit.yaml` and the full manuscript. Each paragraph need not be fair, complete, self-contained and pedagogically closed; the book as a whole must be accurate and coherent within the stance, scope and structure the user asked for. Decide candidate by candidate: delete redundant, merge duplicated, move misplaced, consolidate important repetition, keep useful, keep what the user asked for, and keep genre-required material unless the user excluded it.

The goal is the user's book with less mechanical repetition and over-explanation — not a normalised, balanced or "humanised" one. Do not restore counterarguments the user did not want repeated, add summaries or teaching scaffolding they excluded, soften a stance they asked for, or standardise a structure they chose. Fairness here means not misrepresenting sources, not adding balance the user declined.

Make the smallest change that fixes the reader problem. Preserve the author's voice, chapter contracts, stable IDs, figure references, provenance and citations. Record `intent_check` in `plan/prose-editing.yaml` when the user wrote instructions. Mark each edit's `citation_impact` as `unchanged`, `changed` or `uncertain`. For every changed or uncertain cited claim, compare the revised claim with the original source and record a supported citation re-audit with source IDs in `plan/prose-editing.yaml`. If support is uncertain, repair or revert the edit before completing this phase; the final fact/citation audit then re-reads changed chapters.
