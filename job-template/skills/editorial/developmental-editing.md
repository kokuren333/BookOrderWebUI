# Developmental editing

Work from `plan/prose-audit.yaml` and the full manuscript. Each paragraph need not be fair, complete, self-contained and pedagogically closed; the book as a whole must be. Decide candidate by candidate: delete redundant, merge duplicated, move misplaced, consolidate important repetition, keep useful, and keep genre-required material.

Make the smallest change that fixes the reader problem. Preserve the author's voice, chapter contracts, stable IDs, figure references, provenance and citations. Mark each edit's `citation_impact` as `unchanged`, `changed` or `uncertain`. For every changed or uncertain cited claim, compare the revised claim with the original source and record a supported citation re-audit with source IDs in `plan/prose-editing.yaml`. If support is uncertain, repair or revert the edit before completing this phase; the final fact/citation audit then re-reads changed chapters.
